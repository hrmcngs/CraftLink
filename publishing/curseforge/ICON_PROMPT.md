## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original square PNG project logo for the CraftLink mod on CurseForge, legible as a tiny avatar. Primary request: design one premium, simple, memorable emblem representing a smartphone touch controller linked to a voxel cube. Subject: a chunky square-edged smartphone silhouette with a small original four-direction touch control on its screen, interlocked with a single clean isometric cube through a strong link-shaped connection. Composition: a single unified centered icon filling about 78% of the square, generous edge margins, no lettering. Style: crisp geometric pixel-inspired graphic, restrained subtle depth, clean silhouettes, no tiny decorative details. Background: rich dark charcoal with a restrained grid of large dark tiles, not a blank solid or gradient image. Contrasting light screen and luminous green/cyan connection accents, polished and friendly. Constraints: absolutely no Minecraft logo, no grass-block texture, no Creeper, no existing game characters or assets, no other company logos, no trademarked art, no watermarks, no text or initials; original shapes only. Output one finished square logo, no mockup and no contact sheet, opaque background, suitable for a 1024x1024 PNG.
